# Device artwork in ASO Studio

- `iphone-11-pro-space-gray.svg` and `ipad-pro-12-9-space-gray.svg` come from [PommePlate](https://github.com/ephread/PommePlate), licensed CC0 1.0. The license text is in `POMMEPLATE-LICENSE.txt`. The screenshot is composited over each artwork's screen opening during preview and PNG export.
- `iphone-16-max.svg` and `pixel-9-pro.svg` are extracted from the [MIT-licensed telephone project](https://github.com/sneas/telephone). The first is a stylized iPhone 16 Pro Max front frame (called `iphone-16-max` upstream); the second is a Pixel 9 Pro frame. The license text is in `TELEPHONE-LICENSE.txt`. Their transparent screen opening is composited over the screenshot.
- The iPhone 17 Pro and Galaxy S25 Ultra front frames are local vector approximations informed by the [MIT-licensed react-mockframe device styles](https://github.com/mbdev3/react-mockframe). They are identified as 2D mockups, not official product artwork.
- Older generic frames remain available to existing projects but are hidden from the add menu.
- This repository also contains CC BY 4.0 iPhone and Galaxy 3D models in `public/phone-mockup-studio/source/`. Their authors and source links are documented in `public/phone-mockup-studio/CREDITS.md`. Those GLB models are used by Phone Mockup Studio, not by the ASO SVG/PNG renderer.

Device names belong to their respective owners. This project is not affiliated with Apple or Samsung.
